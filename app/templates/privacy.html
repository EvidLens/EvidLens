EvidLens Ltd. (“EvidLens”, “we”, “us”) operates EvidLens, a Kenya decision-intelligence platform. This Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and your rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (“KDPA”), the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, and the Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021. It applies to our website, web app, Ask Lens AI, APIs, reports, alerts, and support.

Summary in 30 seconds: We collect what you give us (account, searches, prompts, payments), what your device sends (IP, device, logs), and public market data. We use it to run EvidLens, bill you via M-Pesa, generate AI insights, prevent fraud, and improve the product. We do NOT sell your personal data. We do NOT store your M-Pesa PIN. We do NOT train public AI models on your private prompts without consent. You can access, correct, delete, export, or object at any time via dpo@evidlens.co.ke.

3. Legal Basis - KDPA Section 30

We process personal data only on a lawful basis:

  1. Consent (Sec 30(1)(a)) - marketing, analytics cookies, optional AI improvement. Withdraw anytime.
  2. Contract (Sec 30(1)(b)) - to create your account, provide EvidLens, generate reports, support you.
  3. Legal Obligation (Sec 30(1)(c)) - KRA tax records, ODPC compliance, court orders.
  4. Legitimate Interest (Sec 30(1)(d)) - fraud prevention, security, product improvement, aggregated analytics. Balanced against your rights.
  5. Public Interest - anonymised market statistics for public good.

Where consent is the basis, refusing consent will not block core paid features, except optional features that need it (e.g. marketing emails).

4. How We Use Your Data

  1. Provide and maintain EvidLens, Ask Lens, dashboards, reports, alerts and APIs.
  2. Authenticate you, manage subscriptions, process M-Pesa payments, issue invoices/receipts.
  3. Generate AI insights from your prompts + public market data. Your prompts are used to answer you, not to train a public model.
  4. Personalise results by county, sector, budget and history you provided.
  5. Detect fraud, abuse, bot / scraping, and enforce Terms and fair-use / credit limits.
  6. Send service messages: receipts, expiry, alerts you enabled, security notices.
  7. Improve EvidLens via aggregated, de-identified analytics - e.g. “Avocado demand rose in Nyeri” - never “John searched X”.
  8. Comply with KDPA, KRA, ODPC, and lawful requests from Kenyan authorities.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do NOT share your private prompts with advertisers.

5. AI, Ask Lens & Groq - Read This

What happens when you use Ask Lens: Your prompt + relevant context (county, sector, budget you entered) is sent securely to our AI processor (Groq Inc) to generate an answer. Groq does not retain prompts to train its models under our agreement, and we do not send your name, email, phone, or payment data to Groq - only the prompt and market context.

Do not paste: ID numbers, PINs, passwords, bank details, health data, or other people's private data into Ask Lens. If you do, email the DPO and we will delete that chat history.

AI accuracy: AI answers are decision support, not professional advice. Verify prices, regulations, and financials before acting. Financial estimates are labelled “Estimated” where no live DB data exists.

Chat history: Stored in your account so you can revisit it. Delete it anytime from your account, or ask the DPO to delete it. Deleted chats are removed from active systems within 30 days.

9. Security (KDPA Sec 41)

  1. TLS/HTTPS in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, hashed passwords (bcrypt), session tokens.
  2. Role-based access - staff see only what they need. 2FA for staff admin access.
  3. Daily backups, audit logs for report generation / downloads / payments.
  4. Rate limiting, fraud / abuse detection, and logging of suspicious activity.
  5. No system is 100% secure - if you suspect compromise, email dpo@evidlens.co.ke immediately and change your password.

11. Your Rights Under KDPA Section 26

You have the right to:

  1. Be informed - this Policy.
  2. Access - get a copy of your personal data.
  3. Correction - fix inaccurate data.
  4. Deletion / Erasure - delete your account and personal data, subject to tax / legal retention.
  5. Object / Restrict - object to legitimate-interest processing or marketing.
  6. Portability - export your data in a structured format (JSON / CSV).
  7. Withdraw consent - at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing.
  8. Not be subject to solely automated decisions with legal / significant effect - you can ask for human review of an AI verdict.

How to exercise: email dpo@evidlens.co.ke from your registered email, stating the right you want. We verify identity, respond within 21 days, and do not charge unless the request is manifestly unfounded / excessive. To delete your account: Dashboard > Settings > Delete Account, or email the DPO.

EvidLens is for users 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, email the DPO and we will delete it promptly. Parental / guardian consent is required for any user under 18 under KDPA Sec 33.

13. Marketing & Communications

Service messages (receipts, expiry, security, alerts you enabled) are not marketing and cannot be opted out of while you hold an account. Marketing (new features, offers) is opt-in only - you can unsubscribe in any email or by emailing the DPO. We will not send marketing to a number / email on Kenya’s Do-Not-Call / opt-out list.

14. Data Breach Notification

If a breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the ODPC within 72 hours of becoming aware (KDPA Sec 43), and notify affected users without undue delay, describing what happened, what data was affected, and what we are doing. Report a suspected breach to dpo@evidlens.co.ke.

15. Complaints to ODPC

If we do not resolve your concern, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya - odpc.go.ke - or to a court under KDPA Sec 56. Please contact us first so we can fix it.

16. Changes to This Policy
