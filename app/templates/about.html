Our Mission

To remove guesswork from business in Kenya. A farmer in Nyeri, a shop owner in Kisumu, and a manufacturer in Nairobi should all get the same quality of market intelligence that big corporates pay consultants millions for - in minutes, in KES, for their county.

No more asking 5 brokers for a price. No more opening a shop where 20 others already sell the same thing. No more missing a county tender because you heard too late.

The problem we solve

  • • Prices are hidden - you don't know what maize sells for in Eldoret vs Nyeri today.
  • • Demand is invisible - you don't know if people actually want your product in your ward.
  • • Competitors are unknown - you open, then discover 10 others already there.
  • • Policy changes late - a new tax / regulation hits and you were not ready.
