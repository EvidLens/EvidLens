Our Mission

To remove guesswork from business in Kenya. A farmer in Nyeri, a shop owner in Kisumu, and a manufacturer in Nairobi should all get the same quality of market intelligence that big corporates pay consultants millions for - in minutes, in KES, for their county.

That means one thing above everything else: evidence before opinion. If the data says your idea is weak in your ward, we tell you. If it says it's strong but only at a different price point, we tell you that too. A "No-Go" that saves you KES 500,000 is worth more than a flattering "Go" that costs you KES 500,000.

We started in Nyeri because that's where the problem is most visible: smart people, good products, terrible information. A dairy farmer doesn't fail because they can't farm. They fail because they didn't know the price in the next town, or that three other cooperatives already supply the same buyer.

OUR PRINCIPLES

  • 1. Evidence before opinion - every verdict shows its sources
  • 2. County before country - national averages hide the truth
  • 3. Honest uncertainty - if we don't know, we say we don't know
  • 4. Action before dashboard - a decision you can act on today

The problem we solve

  • • Prices are hidden - the same bag of maize can sell for a different price in Eldoret, Nakuru, and Nyeri on the same day, and there is no single place to check. You find out by calling 5 brokers, and each one has a reason to lie.
  • • Demand is invisible - you don't know if people in your ward actually want your product, or if they already buy it from someone else. So you open and hope.
  • • Competitors are unknown - you sign a lease, stock up, then discover 10 others already sell the same thing on the same street, at a lower price.
  • • Policy changes late - a new county levy, a KRA rule, a KEBS standard hits and you hear about it from a fine, not from a notice.
  • • Advice is generic - most "business advice" in Kenya is written for Nairobi, or for America. It doesn't know your county, your budget, or your ward.

THE COST OF GUESSING

One wrong launch - wrong product, wrong ward, wrong price - can cost KES 100,000 to KES 500,000 in stock, rent, and lost time. EvidLens exists to make that mistake visible before you spend the money, not after.

That is the whole product. Not a dashboard to stare at. A decision, with evidence, that you can act on today.

How we build an answer - methodology

Every Ask Lens answer follows the same pipeline. No black box.

1. Collect - Live only

Prices from KPIN and market sources, businesses, news, policy, and consumer signals. Collected daily at 03:00 EAT. No seeding, no demo prices.

2. Label - Live / Recent / Estimated

Live: last 48h. Recent: last 30 days. Estimated: modelled from nearest county + last known price, clearly marked. If we have nothing, we show a gap, not a guess.

3. Decide - Go / No-Go

Market size, margin, competition, risk, and policy are scored per county, not nationally. The verdict shows what would change it. If a price drop of 10% flips the answer, we tell you.

Data sources

KPIN market prices, public business listings, KNBS / CBK / KRA public releases, county notices, news, and consumer signals. We do not scrape private WhatsApp groups, we do not buy personal data, and we do not present AI guesses as prices.

Limitations - read this

Prices move daily. A price from yesterday is not a guarantee for tomorrow. Policy changes can be announced late. Estimates are estimates. Always verify final supplier price, lender terms, and county levies before you commit money. EvidLens is decision intelligence, not legal, tax, or financial advice.
