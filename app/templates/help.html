How do I buy credits?

Go to Billing → Buy Credits, choose a pack, pay with M-Pesa. Credits appear instantly. Free = 1 credit, Pro (KES 1,500/mo) = 10 credits, Enterprise (KES 5,000/mo) = unlimited.

What is Demand Score?

A 0-100 score from search interest, competition, and buying signals in your county + sector. 70+ = strong demand, 40-69 = moderate, under 40 = weak.

Why is my report locked?

You are out of credits, or the module needs Pro. Check Billing for your balance and plan.

How do I reset my password?

Go to Login → Forgot password. The link expires in 30 minutes.

Is my data private?

Yes. KDPA 2019 compliant. See Privacy, Terms, and DPA. Your prompts are anonymized before AI inference.

How do refunds work?

Contact support within 7 days of payment. M-Pesa refunds go to the same number used to pay.
